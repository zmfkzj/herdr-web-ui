### Added

- The private desktop viewer has a **Switch input** button (Korean: 입력 전환) that turns mouse and
  keyboard control on and off on the open connection, so a desktop `/gui view` opened view-only
  can be controlled without disconnecting or entering its password. Input goes only after the
  PC confirms the switch, held keys and buttons are let go on the way back to view-only, and a
  device that may only watch is refused. The badge beside the button shows the mode in force.
  A PC whose bridge predates the switch does not confirm it: install this server's Pi visual
  bridge there.
