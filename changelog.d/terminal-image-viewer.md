### Added
- A picture pi draws inline in the terminal (a `read` image, `generate_image` output, or a pasted
  image before and after it is sent) opens on click in the image viewer, as the chat's do: enlarge,
  download, and pi's image comments. Pasted pictures show at full resolution in the terminal with
  the pi images extension's `file:` terminal references; a watching device cannot read them.
